# Author–AC Confidential Comment

Dear Area Chair,

We respectfully ask that the reviews be reconsidered in the context of
biomedical research. We accept the technical limitations: two datasets
cannot isolate workflow variability from all confounders, and the
five-fold effect is modest and heterogeneous. Our concern is that the
paper is also being judged against expectations developed for
general-purpose, web-scale datasets.

Surgical video contains sensitive patient information. Multicenter data
collection requires ethics approval, consent or waiver,
de-identification, institutional agreements, secure access, and costly
clinical annotation. NeurIPS's own record reflects this scarcity: among
19,024 papers in the 2021–2025 proceedings, a conservative title audit
found only eight biomedical-surgery papers (0.042%), only five in the
Datasets & Benchmarks track, and none on remaining-surgery-duration
prediction.

The E&D track welcomes evaluation-methodology contributions and does not
require every paper to release a new unrestricted dataset or architecture.
Our contributions are a future-frame-safe evaluation protocol,
separation of retrospective-oracle from prefix-derived conditioning, a
shuffled-label semantic control, and transparent within-center,
cross-center, and five-fold analyses.

We are not requesting relaxed technical standards. We ask that dataset
scale and availability be evaluated in their medical-governance context,
and that the paper be assessed on whether it improves how evaluative
claims are constructed and interpreted. Equating significance with
web-scale data would unintentionally favor well-resourced organizations
and discourage biomedical communities whose data are hardest to collect
responsibly. We would appreciate your asking the reviewers to reconsider
their assessments under this domain-aware framing.

Thank you for your consideration.
