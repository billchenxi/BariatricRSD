# Manuscript Exports

Current manuscript deliverables are grouped into four output bundles. Each
bundle has a PDF and a DOCX companion.

## Output bundles

| Bundle | PDF | DOCX | Purpose | Pages | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Full | `review_manuscript.pdf` | `review_manuscript.docx` | Full version with everything included | 40 | 670 KB / 445 KB |
| Short combined | `review_manuscript_short.pdf` | `review_manuscript_short.docx` | Autocut version with short main text plus references and appendices combined | 30 | 541 KB / 341 KB |
| Main only | `review_manuscript_short_main.pdf` | `review_manuscript_short_main.docx` | Main-paper candidate only | 8 | 194 KB / 88 KB |
| Supplementary only | `review_manuscript_short_supp.pdf` | `review_manuscript_short_supp.docx` | References plus appendices only | 23 | 443 KB / 265 KB |

## Practical note

- `review_manuscript_short_main.pdf` is the length-controlled main-paper
  candidate and currently fits within a 9-page NeurIPS main-text budget.
- `review_manuscript_short_supp.pdf` is split out for convenience only.
  NeurIPS expects references, appendices, and checklist to be included in the
  same submission PDF rather than uploaded as a separate appendix PDF.
